# VoiceInk

**Speak anywhere you type.** VoiceInk is an AI dictation keyboard for iPhone that turns your voice into clean, punctuated text with Google Gemini — right from the keyboard.

- **Speak, don't type.** Dictate emails, notes, and messages faster than thumb-typing.
- **Clean output, not raw transcript.** Gemini removes "um," "uh," and false starts, then adds proper punctuation.
- **Works everywhere.** As a system keyboard, VoiceInk inserts text into any app with a text field.
- **Your key, your control.** Bring your own Google Gemini API key. Audio and text go straight to Google's API and nowhere else.
- **No accounts, no tracking.** No logins, no ads, no third-party analytics.

## How it works

1. Get a free Google Gemini API key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/) and paste it into VoiceInk's settings.
2. Enable the VoiceInk keyboard in iOS Settings and allow microphone access.
3. Switch to the VoiceInk keyboard, tap the mic, and speak.
4. Your words appear as clean text at the cursor.
